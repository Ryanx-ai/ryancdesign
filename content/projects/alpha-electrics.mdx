## A system, not just a battery

As co-founder and Lead Product Designer, Ryan led product, UI/UX and marketing for Alpha Electrics. The venture designed a battery-swapping ecosystem around AlphaCell, a hermetically sealed battery pack suited to Southeast Asia’s weather.
